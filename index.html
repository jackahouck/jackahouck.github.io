About

I am an undergraduate at Boston College studying mathematics and computer science. My interests include machine learning, natural language processing, quantitative modeling, and the systems used to train and evaluate large models.

I am currently conducting NLP research through an NSF REU at the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee, where I work on incorporating linguistic structure into causal language models.

Selected Work

  • Linguistic Metadata for Language Models. Extended a GPT-2 implementation with token-aligned structural metadata, multi-task prediction, and a multi-GPU training pipeline.
  • HER2 Breast Cancer Image Classification . Developed a ConvNeXt-based pipeline and learned heatmap objective for four-class HER2 scoring from immunohistochemistry images.
  • Options Volatility Surface Modeling . Compared tree-based and neural regressors for implied-volatility prediction using SPY and QQQ options data.
  • Political Ideology Prediction . Built text-classification and named-entity-masking experiments over executive orders and congressional bills.

Research Interests
